Axena Health, Inc. (Axena Health), a medical device company focused on female pelvic health, today announced it has entered into a collaboration with Mayo Clinic to integrate the organization’s educational content on overactive bladder (OAB), into Axena Health’s clinically proven Leva® Pelvic Health System.
